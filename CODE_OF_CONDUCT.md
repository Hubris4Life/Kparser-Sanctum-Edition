# Community conduct

This project welcomes respectful technical discussion from users and contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants are expected to:

- Discuss the work rather than attack the person.
- Provide evidence and reproducible details when disagreeing about parser behavior.
- Respect player privacy and remove private chat, credentials, and identifying data from reports.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and deliberate disruption.
- Follow upstream licenses and credit the work on which a contribution is based.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Serious or private conduct concerns should be sent through a private channel listed by the repository owner rather than posted publicly.
